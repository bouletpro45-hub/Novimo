Prices and quotes

How much does an animation cost?

An animated logo costs $337, a short ad $167, the launch pack (logo + ad) $447, a 30-second spot $857 and a 60-second spot $1,697. The logo system (your custom logo and its 5 versions) costs $457. Ready-made templates start at $97. That is at least half the price a freelance motion designer starts at for the same work. All prices are in US dollars.

Is the quote free?

Yes, always. You build your quote online in one minute, you receive it as a PDF by email, and it commits you to nothing until you have signed it.

Why a 30% deposit?

The deposit, paid when you sign the quote, books your slot and starts the work. The balance (70%) is only paid when you approve the final video.

How can you be half the price of a freelancer?

Our animations are built on tools and templates we developed ourselves: we save time on the technical side and pass the savings on to you. The quality stays the same: Full HD video, ready to post.

Timings and revisions

How quickly do I get my video?

For short formats (logo, ad, template), you get a first preview within 48 hours of the deposit and receiving your assets, or within 24 hours with the “Express delivery” option. 30 and 60-second spots have a lead time shown on the quote.

What if I don’t like the video?

Two rounds of revisions are always included: colours, text, pace, music… We adjust until it feels like you. You can order extra revisions if needed.

I don’t have a logo, is that a problem?

Not at all. We can animate your brand name with beautiful typography and a symbol that suits your business. Try it on the home page: type your name and see the result straight away.

Formats and files

Which formats will I receive?

An MP4 video ready to post. Short formats are delivered square (1:1) and vertical (9:16) for Instagram, TikTok and stories; 16:9 landscape is an option. Spots are delivered in 16:9 Full HD (1920 × 1080) for YouTube, TV or an in-store screen.

Can I show my ad on TV?

Yes, our spots are in the right format. Note that each channel asks for a “broadcast-ready” file with its own technical rules (and sometimes an approval from the local advertising authority). Tell us before ordering so we prepare the right file.

How do the ready-made templates work?

On the Templates page, pick a video and change the text, colours and font: the preview updates live. Click “Order”: your quote is already filled in with your choices. We then remake the video in high quality, with your logo.

Account and messaging

Do I need an account to order?

No, you can request a quote without an account. The account (free, no password) lets you find all your quotes and write to us through messaging.

How do I send you a message?

Sign in to your account, then open messages. Choose the category of your request (project, template, order status…) and write your message. As soon as we accept the request, you can chat with us directly.

Are my messages private?

Yes. They travel over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and are encrypted in our database: even if someone accessed the database, their content would stay unreadable. Only you and Novimo can read them.

Payment and rights

Do I own the video?

Once paid in full, you can use your video anywhere (social media, website, shop window, TV), with no time limit. The details are in our terms and conditions.

Can I cancel my order?

Before signing the quote, yes, freely. After signing, since the animation is custom-made, the deposit is non-refundable. If there is a problem, write to us: we always find a solution.
