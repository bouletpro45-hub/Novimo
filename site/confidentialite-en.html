3. For how long?

  • Quotes not followed up: 3 years after the last contact, then deleted.
  • Signed quotes and payments: 10 years, as required by the French Commercial Code for accounting records.
  • Account: as long as it is used; deleted after 3 years without signing in, or as soon as you ask.
  • Messaging: 3 years after the last message, or as soon as you delete your account.
  • Audience measurement: 13 months for the identifier in your browser, 25 months for statistics.
  • Anti-spam IP fingerprint: 30 days.

4. Who has access?

Only Novimo, and the technical providers the website needs, who act on its instructions (processors under the GDPR):

  • Supabase: database and accounts, servers located in the European Union;
  • Brevo (France): sending emails and the PDF quote;
  • Google: only if you choose “Continue with Google”, and to display fonts (Google Fonts);
  • [Hébergeur du site, ex. Netlify, Inc.]: hosting the pages.

Some of these providers are American. Any transfers outside the European Union are covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

5. How your data is protected

The website is only served over HTTPS (encrypted connection). In the database, each client can only read their own quotes; only the administrator account sees everything. Messages are encrypted (AES-256) before being stored: the database only holds unreadable text, and the key stays on the server. Secret keys (email sending, administration, encryption) are never sent to your browser.

6. Your rights

At any time you can ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict its use, object to it, get it back in a readable format (portability), withdraw your consent to audience measurement, and give instructions about it after your death.

Write to bouletpro45@gmail.com: you will get an answer within one month at most. If you think your rights are not respected, you can complain to the CNIL (the French data protection authority) or to the authority of your own country.
